Word Pairs and Explanation:

1.Purchase – Buy
Both words are used when someone acquires something by paying money.
2.Rapid – Swift
These words describe something happening very quickly or at high speed.
3.Silent – Quiet
Both refer to the absence of noise or very low sound.
4.Difficult – Challenging
These words describe something that requires effort and is not easy to do.
5.Ancient – Old
Both are used to describe something that belongs to the past or has existed for a long time.
